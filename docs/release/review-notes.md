# 심사 안내 초안

출시 주체와 의료 앱 적격성 근거를 확정한 뒤 제출용으로 편집한다. 기관 승인/규제 승인을 현재 확보했다고 주장하지 않는다.

The app is a free, offline adult calculation aid for healthcare professionals. It includes bundled content; no login is required. No advertising, payment, analytics, or patient data upload is implemented. Temporary cases are in-memory only. The public web edition has separate local-storage behavior described in the privacy policy.

Calculation checks:

1. Enter weight 60 kg and height 170 cm in Sedative. With Midazolam 0.2 mg/kg/hr and 600 mg in a final 500 mL, the result is 10 mL/hr.
2. Select Propofol for comparison and enter its separate mixture: 1000 mg in 100 mL. The 50 mcg/kg/min row is 18 mL/hr.
3. Open ASM loading. Levetiracetam 60 mg/kg at 60 kg gives 3600 mg; at 100 kg the cap is 4500 mg. Fosphenytoin is explicitly labeled in mg PE.
4. Open Scores. STESS age ≥65, no seizure history, NCSE in coma and stupor/coma yields 6/6.
5. Evidence shows references and calculation formulas. App information links to the bundled privacy and support pages. Calculation continues without network access.
